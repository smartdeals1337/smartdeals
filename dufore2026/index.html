Rules

  1. 4 rounds on Nov 11, 12, 13 and 14, 18 holes each, same course: PGA Riviera Maya, par 72.
  2. Double bogey is the maximum. Pick up at par + 2. The hole counts as par + 2, never more.
  3. Happy ball: one happy ball on hole #1 of every round, only one.
  4. Mulligans: one per 9 holes, so two in total over 18. They are not cumulative: an unused mulligan on the front 9 does not carry over to the back 9.
  5. Add up your 18 holes. That is your gross score.
  6. After the round, subtract your full handicap. That is your net score for the round.
  7. Your tournament total is the sum of your 4 net scores. Lowest total wins the Dufore Cup.
  8. Bonus points: Longest Drive and Closest to the Pin (on a par 3) are played on selected holes, to be announced. Win one and earn 1 point each. Every point lowers your net score by 1.
  9. Hole in one: any hole in one during the tournament automatically wins the Dufore Cup, whatever the standings. (Slugger won Las Vegas 2021 this way.)
  10. Tied? An executive decision within the executive committee determines the winner, taking into account the behaviour of the week, among other things.
