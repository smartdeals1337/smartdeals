Scoring

  1. 4 rounds, 18 holes each, same course: PGA Riviera Maya, par 72.
  2. Double bogey is the maximum. Pick up at par + 2. The hole counts as par + 2, never more.
  3. Add up your 18 holes. That is your gross score.
  4. After the round, subtract your full handicap. That is your net score for the round.
  5. Your tournament total is the sum of your 4 net scores. Lowest total wins the Dufore Cup.
  6. Bonus points: win Longest Drive or Closest to the Pin on a par 3 and earn 1 point each. Every point lowers your net score by 1.
  7. Tied? The better net score in the latest round ranks higher.
